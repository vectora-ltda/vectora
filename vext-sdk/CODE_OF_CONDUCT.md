# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to keep the VEXT SDK welcoming, respectful, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Use constructive language, respect different backgrounds, and focus feedback on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the security or support channels. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
